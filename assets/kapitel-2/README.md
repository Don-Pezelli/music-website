## Content and navigation

All five lessons, reading exercises, hearing exercises, supplied solutions and final learning goals are in `../../kapitel-2.html`. Course navigation includes chapter 2; chapter 1 links forward to it. Solutions are native, keyboard-accessible HTML details elements; checklists use labeled native checkboxes. No audio or exercises were invented. Google Drive links open externally; their recording contents and public-access settings are not independently verified.

Minor spelling/grammar errors are corrected. The missing beat number in lesson 2's play-along instruction is restored to “Schlag 2,” consistent with the two-beat half note. Lesson 1 has no separate hearing exercise in the PDF, so none was added.

The author-only drafting note on page 14 is retained here rather than published as learner-facing instructions: “NOTIZ: erkläre wie man schnell und einfach Rhythmus notiert. Wie an der Uni. Welche Notation hörst du?” No additional explanation was fabricated.

## Figures

All 51 embedded source images were extracted without resizing: 26 QR codes and 25 instructional figures. PNGs retain original channels, alpha masks where present, dimensions and embedded ICC profiles. The PDF download is byte-identical to the source.

Twenty complete rhythm-score diagrams were transcribed to SVG using the Bravura outlines already used by this course. Beam groupings, notes, rests, measure counts and exercise/solution distinctions follow the source. `score-data.json` retains the explicit durations and beam groups; `../../scripts/build-chapter-2-scores.rb` rebuilds the SVG files. All 66 complete measures total four quarter-note beats. The four colored note-value diagrams reuse the existing introduction SVGs, including the user's eighth-note positioning adjustment.

The deliberately incomplete fill-in exercise (PDF object 160) remains its original **1462 × 512 PNG**. Its blanks and note positions were not inferred or completed. It is displayed without destructive resizing and can be opened at original resolution.

Twenty-six QR codes were decoded and rebuilt as exact square-module SVGs. Their 6,205,000 source pixels were compared with the SVG modules. `links.json` maps PDF image object numbers to the decoded URLs. `manifest.json` records all image dimensions and page membership.

Critical mappings: page 7 hearing tasks 1–3 use objects 54/56/58, solutions 55/57/59. Page 14 tasks 1–4 use 133/135/137/138. Page 18 solutions 1/2/3 are objects **187/188/186**, based on their page positions, not PDF object order.
